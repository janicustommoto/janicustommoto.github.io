Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers the Jani Custom Moto website and "Jani Autopost", the small tool Jani Custom Moto (Sabzazar, Lahore) uses to publish its own posts to its own social media accounts.

Website

This website does not use forms, accounts, cookies or tracking. It does not collect personal information. When you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.

Jani Autopost

Jani Autopost is used only by Jani Custom Moto, for its own accounts. It does not access, collect or store data about other people.

  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta APIs): used only to publish posts to the Jani Custom Moto Facebook Page and Instagram account.
  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): used only to upload videos and set video details on the Jani Custom Moto YouTube channel.

The access tokens for these accounts are stored only on Jani Custom Moto's own computer. They are not shared with or sold to anyone. Jani Custom Moto can revoke this access at any time, and Google access can be removed from the Google security settings page.

Jani Autopost uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
